What we do

Six areas of specialised support.

01

AHPRA application concierge

Your registration application prepared, checked and managed from first document to decision.

02

PESCI preparation & exemption assessment

Structured interview preparation, or an honest assessment of whether an exemption applies.

03

Supervision plans (SPPA-30)

Supervision plans drafted to the Board’s requirements, including level changes as you progress.

04

Medicare 19AB / 19AA exemptions

Provider-number exemption applications prepared with the evidence Medicare expects to see.

05

Fellowship pathway documents

Document support for RACGP and ACRRM fellowship pathways, including the FSP: complete, consistent and on time.

06

Practice IMG-readiness, compliance & recruitment

Audits, supervisor matching, ongoing compliance management and compliance-first GP recruitment for practices employing IMGs.

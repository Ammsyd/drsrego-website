## About the role

An established general practice in Kingston, in Logan City, is looking for two full-time vocationally registered GPs for the long term. The engagement is as an independent contractor, on terms reported to us by the practice.

GP couples looking for a long-term move are especially welcome, and individual applicants are just as welcome. One of the two positions takes over an established patient base from a departing doctor.

The practice is in a busy shopping-centre location, with a pharmacy next door and easy parking. It is roughly 30 minutes from the Brisbane CBD and roughly 45 minutes from the Gold Coast.

## About the practice

The practice has been running since 2014 and has nine GPs. On site there are two full-time nurses, four administrative staff and a dietitian.

What the practice says it offers:

- An established patient base to take over from a departing doctor
- Teaching of medical students and GP registrars
- Skin cancer training and mentoring
- A pathway to shareholding in the practice

The practice offers a guaranteed hourly rate for the first six months, moving to a share of billings after that, and a bonus on completing a twelve-month full-time contract. The figures are discussed with you by the practice directly.

## What the practice is looking for

- Vocationally registered GPs, FRACGP or FACRRM
- Specialist Pathway doctors assessed by the RACGP as substantially comparable
- Doctors who want a long-term position rather than a short placement

Internationally trained doctors who are subject to Medicare location restrictions are welcome to enquire. The eligibility note above sets out where this location sits. We work out your own position before anything is put to the practice, and we tell you plainly what we find. Whether a restriction applies to you, and whether an exemption is available for this location, is decided by the Department of Health, Disability and Ageing and Services Australia, not by Drs Rego or the practice.

## How we work

Before any doctor is put forward, we check registration, fellowship and Medicare position against this practice's own address. Nothing that identifies you goes to the practice without your written consent, and first introductions are anonymised. Drs Rego charges doctors no fee at any stage.

Enquiries to info@drsrego.com.au.

Sponsorship, if relevant, is handled by a registered migration agent. Drs Rego does not provide immigration or visa advice.

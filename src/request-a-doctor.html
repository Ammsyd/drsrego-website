For practices

Request a doctor for your practice.

Tell us about the role. We verify your location’s DPA and MMM status and each doctor’s section 19AB position before we shortlist, so we never send you a doctor you cannot legally engage.

What happens next:

  • We check your street address on the Health Workforce Locator and confirm your DPA and MMM classification in writing.
  • We confirm supervision capacity, because a doctor on limited or provisional registration cannot start without an approved supervisor.
  • We introduce only doctors whose registration and Medicare position fits the role. No guarantees on timing: Ahpra, the Colleges and Services Australia make the decisions.

Fields marked *with an asterisk are required.

# SEO audit: drsrego.com.au

Audited 14 September 2026 on the `feature/recruitment-jobs-insights` branch (built output in `_site/`), plus live checks against https://drsrego.com.au. Scope: every page, including a sample job page and a sample Insights post rendered from drafts.

**Summary.** The technical foundation is strong: every page has a unique title, a description, a canonical link, Open Graph tags, one H1, described images with dimensions, and valid structured data. Internal links resolve. The live domain redirects http and www correctly in a single hop. The site's biggest SEO asset is not yet published: the three Insights posts answer the exact questions doctors and practice managers search for, and they sit in draft until the `[VERIFY]` markers are resolved. The three things that will move the needle are listed at the end.

No SEO tool (Ahrefs, Semrush) is connected, so there is no keyword volume or ranking data in this audit. Google Search Console is the place to check impressions and clicks after merge.

## 2. Needs your decision

| Decision | What it is | My recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Publish the three Insights posts** | They are the only pages targeting the question searches ("does fellowship end the moratorium", "19AA vs 19AB", "how to check DPA"). They stay hidden until you resolve each `[VERIFY]` marker. | Do this first. Most claims are drawn from health.gov.au pages I read on 14 September 2026, so verification should take an hour, not a day. |
| **Analytics** | ~~The site has no analytics at all.~~ **Done 16 September 2026:** Google Analytics 4 is wired in, along with an event that records which "Ask us" button was clicked. It is switched off until you paste your Measurement ID into `src/_data/site.json`. The privacy policy describes it. | Create a free GA4 property at analytics.google.com, copy the Measurement ID (it looks like `G-XXXXXXXXXX`), and send it to me or paste it into `analyticsId` in `src/_data/site.json`. Until then nothing is collected. |
| **Google Business Profile** | **A listing already exists.** Google Maps shows Drs Rego as a Consultant with the website, phone and opening hours. | Claim it if you have not: search "Drs Rego" on Google Maps and click **Claim this business**. Claiming lets you control the description, hours, photos and reviews. |
| **Founder bio on the About page** | The About page still has placeholder "small team" copy and a stock photo. Google's quality guidelines weigh who is behind advice on health-adjacent topics. | A real name, photo and two paragraphs of background. This also strengthens the `author` on every post. |
| **Clean URLs (`/for-doctors/` instead of `/doctors.html`)** | The nav says "For Doctors" but the address says `doctors.html`. Moving would need 301 redirects and a temporary dip while Google re-indexes. | Not worth it. The words in the URL carry almost no ranking weight and Netlify already serves `/doctors`. Leave it. |
| **Self-hosted fonts** | Google Fonts CSS is the one render-blocking request. Self-hosting Inter would remove a third-party round trip and improve Largest Contentful Paint slightly. | Nice to have. Do it only if PageSpeed Insights shows LCP over 2.5 seconds on mobile after launch. |
| **Privacy policy update** | The consent wording on the doctor form covers anonymised profiles sent to practices; the policy does not describe that use yet. Not an SEO issue, but a trust and compliance one. | Lawyer pass before the doctor form goes live. |
| **Remove the "Template for review" notice** | The privacy and terms pages show a visible box saying to have them checked by a lawyer. That was on the live site before this work. | Remove once the lawyer has reviewed. |

## 3. Out of scope for now

- **Content Security Policy header.** Calendly and Google Fonts make a strict policy fiddly, and the gain is security hardening, not search. Revisit if the site ever handles more than forms.
- **Keyword research and competitor benchmarking.** No SEO tool is connected, and the site's target queries are narrow and well understood (19AB, DPA, PESCI, supervision, IMG recruitment). Revisit when Search Console has three months of data.
- **Image CDN or AVIF conversion.** Images are already small and responsive.
- **Hreflang, pagination, faceted navigation.** Not applicable to a site this size.
- **Link building.** The only link-worthy asset is the Insights content; publish it first.
- **Google Jobs appearance.** JobPosting data is in place. Google decides whether to show roles, and it needs real adverts to index. Test the first real advert with the Rich Results Test after it goes live.

## The three fixes that will actually move the needle

1. **Publish the Insights posts and share each one on LinkedIn.** Three posts that answer the questions your audience types into Google, with sources, and a share image that renders correctly. Everything else on this list is housekeeping compared with this.
2. **Put a real person on the About page.** Name, photo, background. Doctors deciding whether to trust a consultant with their registration will look for it, and search engines increasingly do too.
3. **Connect the repository to Netlify (if it is not already) and resubmit the sitemap in Search Console after merge.** This keeps the sitemap, structured data and redirects live automatically, so nothing on this list decays.

## Technically correct but not worth your time this quarter

- Clean URL migration.
- Self-hosting fonts.
- Content Security Policy.
- FAQ schema tuning. Google restricted FAQ rich results to government and health authority sites in 2023, so the FAQ markup on the doctors and practices pages is harmless but earns no visible result. Leave it.
- Sitemap priorities and change frequencies. Google ignores them.
- Schema for services or offers. The enquiry-only model means there is nothing to mark up without stating prices.
